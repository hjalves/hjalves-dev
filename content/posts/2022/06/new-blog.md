Hello! This is my new blog.
It uses the [Hugo static site generator](https://gohugo.io/), and a theme _inspired_
in an older version of [Tania Rascia's website](https://taniarascia.com)
available here: https://github.com/WingLim/hugo-tania.

My previous blog was based on [Pelican](https://blog.getpelican.com/), and although I
really loved the fact that it was written in Python, I needed to try something new to
keep me interested. I only had a couple of really old blog posts, written in Portuguese.
I feel that they're not that relevant and useful anymore, so I decided to simply delete
them.

I'm going to try to write exclusively in English this time, because even though I'm
terrible at expressing myself in English (and in general), I hope I can improve my
writing skills while doing so.

Hope there's any relevant or interesting content to you, my dear reader! 😃
